EXPERIMENT 3: Lowercasing Side Effects (`line.lower()` vs raw case)
======================================================================
[FLORES English]
  Tokens with lower(): 27994 | Tokens without lower(): 27044
  Delta: -950 (-3.39%)
  Mechanism: In English, GPT-2 has distinct tokens for capitalized words; lowercasing artificially
             reduces English token count by 3.39%. In Indic scripts, casing does not exist,
             so lower() is a no-op on native script but distorts mixed English/acronyms.
